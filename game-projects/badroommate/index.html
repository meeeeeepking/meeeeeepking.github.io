Bad Roommate

Group Size: 4

Role: Programmer / Level Designer

About

Bad Roommate is a game I helped make for the RIT Game Dev Club's Haloween Game Jam 2025. The game is made in godot and is a game about being the worst roommate by hiding the items that your roommate needs to get their chores done and get to class on time. The game uses 2d sprites placed in the 3d world giving it a unique art style.

Gameplay

You play as a bad roommate and must move the various items that your roommate needs to finish their chores to stall him long enough that he is late for class. You can see a list of tasks your roommate has left to track what they are currently trying to do. You can move any of the items around but make sure not to get caught by your roommate. This makes the gameplay mainly revolve around stealth.

What I Did

I was one of 3 programmers on this project and was mainly in charge of the level design by taking the sprites made by the artist and puting them into the eviorment to create the level. I also handled things like the camera and sounds since I was the one with the most free time to help where it was needed.

What I Learned

my main learning point here was how to make tools for the engine since I made a tool script to help make the rooms since we wern't sure about the size of the rooms at first and manualy changing the size of every wall and the celieng and floor each time we wanted a size change was unfeesible. It also helped make the doorways and handle different camera positions for each room. The other major learning point of this project was collaboration in the code since none of us were very used to collaborating with other programmers in real time so we had several times where merge conflict occured but we got better at avoiding such things by the end of the project.
