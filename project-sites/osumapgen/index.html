OSU! Map Generator

Group Size: Solo

Role: Sole Developer

About

note: this is a in progress project that has not yet been completed and is still in its early phases

the idea for this project is to train a generative AI that can take in any piece of music and turn it into a map for the rythm game OSU!. as of writing this, i am in the process of creating a program to process the training data for the AI from its current form into a form that the AI can use. more specificaly, my training data will be existing maps, which i will be extracting the placement of the circles, sliders, and spinners from and extracting the associated music track and processing it into a more usefull form. this information will be used to train the AI to create the map.
