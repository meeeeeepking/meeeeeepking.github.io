RIT World Building Club: Magitech vs Old Magic

Group Size: 3

Role: Lead World Builder

About

As part of the RIT Would Building Club I lead the creation of a piece of the club world based on the concept of a battle between a country with high fantasy magic and a country with a form of technological harnesing of magic we called magitech. The wourld building of this world was focuced on larger organizations and history rather than individuals with there being no named person from the world in our world building documents. The actual documentation for the world is on world anvil along with the other parts of the wider 2025-2026 club world which is linked at the bottom of this site.

creative colaboration

most world building work was done durring club meetings with the whole group together. we would come up with ideas, bounce them off of eachother to refine them, and then write them down in the world documentation steadily creating the many organizations and systems that govern our world. as the person who originaly came up with the idea for this world, I became the defacto leader with most descisions on whether a piece of lore fit in with the rest of the world coming down to me, though I did try to take everyone's view points into consideration.

there were some exceptions to this workflow with each of us taking on more in depth pieces of world building that we had more control over except in cases where interacted heavily with the rest of the world. my focus area was the magic system which I designed from the ground up in a way that it feels almost more like a science than a form of magic.

Artifacts

to display our world building to others, we created artifacts which are objects that represent parts of our world. since these artifacts were made towards the end of the school year, i was the main person making them for my group since the rest were much more busy at the time. our artifacts were: a 3d print of a sky lily which is a type of magical plant, a 3d print of a simple spell that creates a warm breeze, and the schematic for a resonator which is the spell that is at the core of all magitech.

What I Learned

throuhgout this project I gained experience on what it is like to colaborate on a highly creative project. I learned how to build a coherent world while working with a group of people all of which have different ideas.
